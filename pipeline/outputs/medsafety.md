# Medication safety net: evaluation

Generated by `dev/medsafety_eval.py`. The rule and the dose extractor were fixed from the training set before being scored on the validation calls.

## 1. The rule on the 500 training calls

- Drugs said in a call (formulary matches): 497. The rule calls 405 of them *expected* (the caller said it, with a number or in two or more turns).
- Of the expected drugs, **342 (84%)** are recorded as a typed medication fact in the training gold.
- Of the gold's medication facts that a formulary match can find, **336 of 371 (91%)** are expected drugs.

## 2. Dose fill on the training gold (dose blanked, then filled from the bullet text)

- Medication facts with a dose (with or without a separate strength): 363. The extractor fills 338 (93%); **329 of them (97%) equal the gold dose and unit**.
